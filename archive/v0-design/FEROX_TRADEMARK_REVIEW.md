# Ferox Trademark Review

This document records a first-pass trademark screening review for the name **Ferox**.

This is **not legal clearance**.

Review date:

* March 8, 2026

Current scope:

* United States
* Brazil
* first-pass screening based on publicly accessible trademark sources and linked public references

## Summary

Current conclusion:

* `Ferox` looks **less crowded than `Orbis`**, but it still does **not** look clean enough to treat as a low-risk public language mark
* the United States has active exact `FEROX` filings and a live international `FEROX` registration history
* Brazil already shows exact `FEROX` trademark records in public INPI material, so it should not be treated as an open field

Current recommendation:

* treat `Ferox` as a medium-risk candidate rather than a clean one
* if `Ferox` becomes the preferred replacement name, run a real clearance review before public launch

## Brazil Screening

### 1. Brazil requires its own search and cannot be inferred from U.S. results

Why it matters:

* INPI states that trademark rights are exclusive in Brazilian territory
* INPI recommends prior searching before filing
* this means Brazil needs its own real clearance work rather than a U.S.-only conclusion

Sources:

* INPI FAQ on marcas: https://www.gov.br/inpi/es/servicios/preguntas-frecuentes/marcas
* INPI basic guide: https://www.gov.br/inpi/pt-br/assuntos/marcas
* INPI e-Marcas entry point: https://www.gov.br/inpi/pt-br/assuntos/marcas/e-marcas

### 2. Public INPI material already shows exact `FEROX` filings in Brazil

Notable records:

* `FEROX`
* application numbers `905952359` and `905953223`
* applicant shown as `RTR TECNOLOGIA LTDA. (BR/MG)`
* visible in the INPI Revista da Propriedade Industrial

Why it matters:

* this is direct evidence that the exact word already entered the Brazil trademark system
* one filing is shown for product class coverage and another for service class coverage
* even though this is older material and not a full prosecution/status review, it is enough to reject any assumption that Brazil is clear

Sources:

* INPI RPI PDF: https://revistas.inpi.gov.br/pdf/marcas2216.pdf

## Practical Recommendation

For release planning:

* `Ferox` currently looks better than `Orbis`, but not clean enough to call low-risk
* do not treat Brazil or the United States as open territory without a proper follow-up clearance review
* if the project wants a bolder, meaning-heavy name and accepts some residual risk, `Ferox` is at least more defensible than `Orbis`
