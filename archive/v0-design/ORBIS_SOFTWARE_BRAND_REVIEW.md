# Orbis Software And Developer-Tool Brand Review

This document records a first-pass brand-conflict review for the name **Orbis** in software and developer-tool contexts.

This is **not legal clearance**.

Review date:

* March 8, 2026

## Summary

Current conclusion:

* `Orbis` is already crowded in software and adjacent technical branding
* the exact word is active across software consulting, enterprise software, AI products, apps, SDKs, packages, and developer-facing tooling
* even apart from trademark law, the brand risk is high enough that `Orbis` should not be treated as a clean public-language name

## Notable Conflicts

### 1. `ORBIS SE`

Observed use:

* active global software and business consulting company at `orbis.de`
* software, ERP, MES, SAP, Microsoft, and digitalization services

Why it matters:

* exact-word `ORBIS` brand
* large established software-company footprint
* the company is directly in enterprise software and technical consulting rather than an unrelated sector

Sources:

* https://www.orbis.de/en/company/about-us/
* https://www.orbis.de/en/

## Practical Interpretation

For a public programming-language launch:

* `Orbis` is not a clean brand
* the exact word is already active enough in software and developer contexts that confusion, search dilution, and naming collision are realistic
* this is true even before adding the separate trademark concerns documented elsewhere

Current recommendation:

* acceptable only if you consciously accept branding and legal follow-up risk
* not recommended as a low-risk final public language name
