# T069

Analyze what Ferox should learn from Pony and Verona concurrency ideas without importing full capability machinery.

## Goal

Extract useful concurrency and ownership lessons from Pony and Verona while preserving Ferox's smaller and more explicit core.

## Review Focus

* local reasoning
* isolation and sharing boundaries
* concurrency safety ideas that might transfer without a full capability lattice

## Expected Output

* a recommendation to adopt, adapt, defer, or reject
* a clear statement of what Ferox should learn and what it should avoid copying
