# T070

Analyze whether effect handlers or richer effect systems belong only in a much later Ferox v2+ wave.

## Goal

Confirm whether richer effect systems are valuable enough to keep on the long-term roadmap without letting them back into the v1 core prematurely.

## Review Focus

* long-term value of richer effect systems
* what problems they would actually solve for Ferox
* whether their cost and complexity justify only a later v2+ evaluation

## Expected Output

* a recommendation to keep as v2+, study only, or reject
* a clear statement of what evidence Ferox would need before revisiting them later
