# T067

Analyze whether Perceus-style reuse and in-place compilation ideas should influence Ferox.

## Goal

Determine whether Ferox should borrow compilation and memory-management ideas that improve performance without widening the language surface.

## Review Focus

* reuse analysis
* in-place update opportunities
* whether the idea belongs in implementation strategy rather than source semantics

## Expected Output

* a recommendation to adopt, adapt, defer, or reject
* a clear statement of whether the idea belongs in Ferox v1 implementation, later backend work, or not at all
